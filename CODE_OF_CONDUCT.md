# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good faith, welcome differing
experience levels, keep technical criticism focused on the work, and protect
other people's privacy and security.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, publishing credentials, and sustained personal attacks are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed confidentially and fairly. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close interactions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository interactions and public representation of the
project. Good-faith security research reported through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) is encouraged.
